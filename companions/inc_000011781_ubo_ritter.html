5 · Access — the role to grant

Two actions, not one — node + role
Renata's ADD to the nodes (OOCU_RESP/DBS) must be paired with a Security/PFCG ticket to grant YS:FI:M:BCM_MON_APP______:UBO (the entity-derived BCM approve role: S_TCODE BNK_APP + C_SIGN digital signature + F_STAT_MON + F_REGU_BUK BUKRS=UBO). Being in the node alone does not let her sign — that was her original "not authorized to use transaction BNK_APP" error.
