The Purpose of Payment end-to-end solution at UNESCO. Some countries' central banks will not process an incoming payment unless it states why the money is being sent. UNESCO satisfies that with a custom framework built on three maintained tables and one validation exit: it forces the AP clerk to declare a reason when the invoice is entered, carries that reason through to payment, and assembles it into the exact string the receiving bank expects. Nine countries run on it today.

This page covers the whole chain — what is maintained and what each thing means, what makes a country mandatory, how the string is built, what the nine countries look like in production, and what can silently switch the controls off.

1 · The shape of the solution

The two halves are independent. Capture is enforced by a validation exit; rendering is driven by config. A country can have one without the other.

2 · What is maintained — and what each thing actually is

ObjectWhat it is, in plain termsWho maintains it
T015L
SCB indicator		 The menu of reasons a clerk can choose from. One row per reason, per country. The key LZBKZ is what the clerk types on the invoice (e.g. JO2); ZWCK1 holds the meaning.
This is a standard SAP field — the "State Central Bank indicator" used in German foreign-trade reporting — repurposed by UNESCO as the purpose-code carrier. That is why the name has nothing to do with purposes of payment.		 FI config · SM30 / OBAT
YTFI_PPC_STRUC
"PPC code structure"		 Two jobs in one table. First, its mere existence for a country is what makes that country mandatory — the validation looks here and nowhere else. Second, each row is one building block of the final text, in CODE_ORD sequence: a separator, a fixed word, the code, its description, or a live field from the document. Rows are split by pay type, so a salary run can say something different from a supplier invoice. FI config · SM30 view PPC code structure
YTFI_PPC_TAG
tag mapping		 Where the finished text is placed in the payment file. One row per country and debit/credit, holding the full XML path. Two destinations are in use: RmtInf/Ustrd (what a SWIFT :70 "remittance information" becomes) and InstrForCdtrAgt/InstrInf (SWIFT :72, an instruction to the receiving bank). The bank tells you which one it reads. FI config · SM30
OB28
validation assignment		 Which company codes the rule runs in at all. Not a UNESCO table — standard customizing that binds a validation to a company code and call-up point. At UNESCO the assignment is optional and largely unassigned, which is why the control reaches one institute of nine. FI config · OB28
YXUSER
exception table		 A per-user off switch for certain routines. One row = that user skips that routine entirely. It does not cover the purpose-of-payment check, but it does cover two other blocking validations — see §6. functional admin · SM30
BSEG-LZBKZ
the document field		 Where the clerk's answer is stored, on the vendor line of the invoice. From there it travels to the open item, then to the payment proposal, and finally to the file builder. It is a 3-character field: it holds the key into T015L, never the text itself. the AP clerk, at FB60 / MIRO
The one-line mental model. YTFI_PPC_STRUC decides whether a country must answer and how the answer is worded · T015L is the list of allowed answers · BSEG-LZBKZ is where the chosen answer is written · YTFI_PPC_TAG is where it ends up in the file · OB28 decides who is asked at all.

3 · What makes a country mandatory

One row type is the switch. U917 blocks the posting when the vendor's bank country has any row in YTFI_PPC_STRUC whose PPC_CODE is PPC_VAR or PPC_DESCR. Nothing else switches it on — not T015L, not YTFI_PPC_TAG, not the payment method.
U917, YRGGBS00:1547-1590 — registered as validation UNES, step 012, CHECKID 2UNES###009.

3b · Two countries, two layers — the mechanism behind "SocGen only" claim 494

The framework uses two different countries, and confusing them is why the class is called _FR. The BAdI implementation is chosen by the country of our paying house bank. The configuration rows are chosen by the country of their receiving bank.
FI_CGI_DMEE_EXIT_W_BADI:1-177 · YCL_IDFI_CGI_DMEE_FR CM002 · YCL_IDFI_CGI_DMEE_UTIL CM003, all read from P01.
This is why adding a country needs no ABAP. All three layers are generic — u917 reads LFBK-BANKS, CM002 passes ZBNKS, CM003 filters WHERE LAND1 = IV_LAND1. There is no country literal in any of them, and nine beneficiary countries already resolve through the single _FR class.
And it explains the "SocGen only" scope mechanically, not just by specification. Claim 267 quotes the spec; this is the cause. Payments through a German or Italian house bank select _DE/_IT, which implement GET_CREDIT and never call the PPC builder. Payments through Citi select nothing at all.

4 · How the string is assembled

The trap in T015L. ZWCK1 is not a description field. It is two fields in one, split at the first space: "<bank code> <human text>". PPC_VAR takes the first word, PPC_DESCR takes everything after it. Get the space wrong and the bank receives a mangled code.
YCL_IDFI_CGI_DMEE_UTIL::GET_TAG_VALUE_FROM_CUSTO, P01 lines 91-169.
Block (SM30 name)Emits
SEPARATOR · FIXED_VAL
Separator / Fixed value		the literal in PPC_VALUE, appended as-is
PPC_VAR
PPC variable		T015L by the LZBKZ the clerk typed → first word of ZWCK1
PPC_DESCR
PPC description		same read → everything after the first word
PAY_FIELD
Payment structure + Field		dynamic assign of IS_<PAY_STRUC>-<PAY_FIELD> — a live document field, e.g. FPAYP-XBLNR

P and R use FIXED_VAL in every configured country, because the purpose of a salary run or a treasury transfer is known in advance. Only O carries PPC_VAR — which is precisely why the block exists: the third-party invoice is the one case where a human must decide.

5 · The nine countries as they are actually configured

Egypt is the tenth, and it is different in one way worth noticing. Built in D01 on 2026-08-19 as EG · USTRD · O → /<PPC_DESCR>/INV/<FPAYP-XBLNR>. Every one of the nine below emits the code; Egypt emits only the narrative, because Citi's notice asked for a description and named no code list — so there is no PPC_VAR row and the ISO code stays internal to UNESCO's reporting. Proven output: /Payment for goods or services received/INV/224938. See the request companion §12.

Two tag families: USTRD → RmtInf/Ustrd = SWIFT :70 Remittance Information (seven countries), and INSTRINF → InstrForCdtrAgt/InstrInf = SWIFT :72 instruction to the account-with institution (AE, CN). They are different fields; the bank's own notice tells you which one it wants. AE and CN also carry a -INSTRINF row with pay type All and an empty value — the DMEE suppression node.

6 · What can switch the controls off — the exception table

YXUSER is a per-user bypass, and it is bigger than the documentation said. Every gated routine opens with SELECT SINGLE * FROM yxuser WHERE xtype = '<x>' AND uname = sy-uname then CHECK sy-subrc <> 0 — a row means skip this entire routine for this user. It gates five routines, two of which can block a posting. claim 486
YXUSER content has never been read. We know the door exists; we do not know who holds a key.

7 · Two limits on the whole solution

Enforcement reaches one company code of nine. U917 is step 012 of validation UNES, and OB28 assigns that validation to UNES only. ICTP runs 4 steps, IIEP 2, UBO 3, UIL 1, UIS its own; IBE, ICBA and MGIE have no validation assigned at all. Adding a country switches capture on for UNES and nobody else. claim 485 · KU-2026-099-PPC-INSTITUTE-COVERAGE
Rendering only exists where a file exists. The PPC framework is scoped to the Société Générale CGI file by written specification (PPC spec v2.0 p.16). Citibank payments carry no PPC, and payment methods configured as cheque produce no file at all. Capture and rendering must be reasoned about separately for every country. claim 267 · 488

8 · How to improve this

Seven weaknesses, each one measured while configuring Egypt — ranked by what it would actually prevent. Three are silent: the posting succeeds, the auditor is satisfied, and the bank still gets nothing usable.

#WeaknessMeasuredFix
1 The control checks the field is filled, not that it is right. T015L’s key is LZBKZ alone — it has no country field. The country prefix is a naming convention. u917 only tests non-empty, so JO6 on an Egyptian payment passes every layer and reaches Citi. 73 rows, one flat namespace, 9 prefixes by convention only One extra condition in u917 — lzbkz(2) must equal lv_banks. A line in a FORM we already own. highest value / lowest cost
2 A blocked posting is not a rendered file. The three tables are maintained independently and nothing in SAP ties them together. Load the switch without the tag and users are forced to fill a field that is then never written to the payment file. 9 of 9 countries currently consistent — but by hand, not by construction Landed: Zagentexecution/quality_checks/ppc_country_consistency_check.py — switch without codes / switch without tag / codes without switch / degenerate usage. Runs on the Gold DB, exits non-zero on HIGH.
3 Vendors with no bank record are invisible to the control. u917 does CHECK lv_banks IS NOT INITIAL and exits — so missing master data silently disables the check rather than triggering it. 254 lines on the Egypt-originating population alone Decide the intent: either these are genuinely out of scope (cheques) or the vendor master is incomplete. Today the code cannot tell the difference.
4 There is no emergency bypass — by design. YXUSER gates five routines; u917 is not one of them. measured against the 5 gated routines Nothing to build — but know it before go-live, not at 18:00 on a closing day. The only lever is deleting the YTFI_PPC_STRUC rows.
5 One catch-all code can make the whole control cosmetic. If everyone picks “other”, the field is filled, the validation passes, and the bank data is worthless. IN7 63% · ID7 34% · MY9 33% · MA9 32% — all generic “other business services” Measure the spread after go-live (check D above). A dominant OTHR is a training gap wearing a config gap’s clothes.
6 Only the French house-bank class dispatches PPC. Our bank picks the class, their bank picks the config rows — and YCL_IDFI_CGI_DMEE_DE / _IT / _FALLBACK never call the lookup at all. 3 classes implement GET_CREDIT only; Citi house banks have no implementation Latent: pay a PPC country from a non-FR house bank and nothing renders, with no warning. Worth a monitor before it becomes an incident.
7 The control stops at company code UNES. The validation is registered there and nowhere else. 1,720 lines across the five other institutes A business decision, not a technical one — but it should be a decision, not an accident of where the validation was first assigned.
And on how this kind of request gets handled. What was missing when the Citi notice arrived was not analysis capacity — it was a place where a country’s configuration is written down once. Four measurement errors were made and corrected during this analysis (wrong key, UBNKS misread as the payee’s bank, a sum across currencies, the wrong denominator), and each one survived as long as it did because the facts lived in a conversation instead of an artefact. They now live here and in the claims store. The next country should cost hours, not a session.
